About

I am an undergraduate biochemistry major on the pre-med track at The University of Texas at Dallas with career aspirations toward pediatric medicine. My journey bridges rigorous medical sciences with the transformative, healing capacity of music.

As an All-State musician with over 13 years of experience across piano, guitar, and electric bass, I serve as church and school pianist/accompanist and have mentored 24 individual students in performance and original composition. Driven to merge clinical outreach and music, I founded Healing Measures, organizing student compositions and live recitals for patients and residents in medical and care facilities.
